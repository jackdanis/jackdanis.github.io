About

Hi! I’m Jack.

I’m a graduate of CU Boulder, where I majored in Business Marketing with a minor in Creative Technology and Design.

That means I know the ins and outs of running an efficient system, with the added ability to create some seriously enticing multimedia content too. I’ve worked as a graphic designer, video editor, consultant, data analyst and a caddie.

More about me

This website is pretty cool. How did you make it?

I coded it myself, every little bit! From the navigation menu to the responsive layout, the illustrations and each portfolio page. No Squarespace, GoDaddy or WordPress here.

What are your favorite hobbies?

Biking, hiking and playing video games. I’m currently reading Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and I’m always watching new stand-up.

Where do you live?

Chicago, IL for the time being, but I spend a lot of time in Colorado too.

What kind of work have you done?

A bit of everything. While I studied marketing, I’ve worked as a graphic designer, video editor, consultant, data analyst and a caddie. I especially enjoy the creative side of things, but my programming experience comes in handy too.
