About

Hi! I’m Jack.

I’m an AI Admin at Quad, where I research, design and roll out AI workflow projects across internal teams. I studied Business Marketing at CU Boulder with a minor in Creative Technology and Design.

That mix means I’m comfortable on the business side and in the creative and technical work that produces long-term results. If there’s data behind a business decision, I’m the one picking it apart. I’ve worked with Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, Autodesk Fusion, Blender, HTML/CSS/JS, Google Analytics, Marketo and Salesforce.
